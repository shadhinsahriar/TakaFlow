Local Storage Notice

Cloud backend and account synchronization have not been implemented yet. Your financial records are currently saved locally in this browser. Your data will normally remain available when you reopen TakaFlow in the same browser, but clearing browser data or site storage may permanently delete your records. Data is not synchronized across devices. Export a backup regularly to protect your financial information.
